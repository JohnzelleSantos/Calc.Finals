06 / FINAL REFLECTION

More than just finding an answer.

Looking back at my Calculus activities, I can see that the subject challenged me to be more patient and careful when solving problems. Some activities were difficult at first, especially when several rules could be used in the same expression. With practice, I became more familiar with the process and learned to focus on the steps instead of only the final answer.

I also realized that the skills from Calculus can connect to my IT studies. Being able to recognize patterns, follow logic, and solve problems step by step are useful beyond mathematics. This report is a record of the work I completed and the progress I made while learning the subject.
